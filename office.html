PROTOTYPE Synergix · out of office

A day at the client's

No desk, no phone call. You spend one day at the client's office: a floor plan, a day of eight hours, people who move around, and a meeting at 15:00 with the person who asked and two colleagues who want different things.

Every visit takes an hour. Look at the plan to see who is where. At 15:00 you sit down with all three and try to leave with commitments.

This prototype needs a Gemini key (the same one the main game uses; it stays in this browser).
